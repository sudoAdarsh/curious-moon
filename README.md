# Curious Moon 

This is a fun and intuitive approach to learning PostgreSQL via the real Cassini space mission, a collaboration between organizations such as the European Space Agency and the Italian Space Agency, led by NASA. 

Launched in 1997, it performed a flyby of the Venusian atmosphere to gain a gravity assist, reaching Saturn's sphere of influence in 2008. From that point on, it collected and transmitted hundreds of gigabytes of data. 

It also explores one of Saturn's most popular moons, Enceladus. Enceladus is 313 miles in diameter and is one of the shiniest bodies in our solar system, as it is composed primarily of ice. 

Curious Moon aims to teach PostgreSQL through a fictional story based on a real-world space mission. 

I, for one, am a great fan of the space genre, and there could not be a better resource I could have asked for. 

So let's begin this amazing journey; you can find all related resources here: https://archive.redfour.io/
